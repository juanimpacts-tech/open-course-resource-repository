# AI for Education: GenAI Chatbot Prompt Library

**Creator:** AI for Education  
**Resource Type:** Curated third-party prompt library  
**Audience:** K–12 and higher education educators, administrators, and instructional designers  
**Original Source:** https://www.aiforeducation.io/prompts

## Overview

AI for Education provides a collection of generative AI prompts designed to support educators with instructional planning, assessment development, communication, administrative responsibilities, and professional learning.

The library offers practical examples of how educators can use AI chatbots to support their work.

## Resource Categories

### Lesson Planning
Prompts for developing and adapting instructional activities, differentiating concepts, designing blended learning experiences, and supporting student engagement.

### Assessment
Prompts related to assessment design, evidence of learning, and alternative assessment formats.

### Communication
Prompts for developing communications with students, families, and other education stakeholders.

### Administrative Tasks
Prompts supporting educational planning, scheduling, and administrative workflows.

### Professional Development
Prompts supporting professional learning, coaching, and educator collaboration.

### Student Support
Prompts supporting study planning, tutoring, and differentiated learning activities.

- Faculty professional development workshops

- Exploration of AI-assisted assessment strategies
- Independent experimentation with AI prompts

## Considerations

Educators should review and adapt generated outputs for accuracy, accessibility, bias, privacy, and alignment with institutional policies and learning objectives. Do not enter confidential student information into unapproved tools.
