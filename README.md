# AI Teaching Commons

An open collection of practical resources for educators and students navigating AI in teaching and learning.

## Purpose

AI Teaching Commons helps educators integrate AI into learning environments without sacrificing critical thinking, academic integrity, student agency, or human connection.

- [Student Guides](student-guides/using-ai-for-brainstorming.md)
- [Faculty Guides](faculty-guides/designing-ai-aware-assignments.md)
- [Prompt Library](prompt-library/feedback-prompts.md)
- [Classroom Assignments](assignments/ai-output-critique.md)
- [Responsible AI](responsible-ai/verification-checklist.md)

## Who Is This For?

Educators, students, instructional designers, and anyone interested in thoughtful AI integration in education
