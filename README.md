# AI Teaching Commons

An open collection of practical resources for educators and students navigating AI in teaching and learning.

## Purpose

AI Teaching Commons helps educators integrate AI into learning environments without sacrificing critical thinking, academic integrity, student agency, or human connection.

## Explore the Resources

- **[Student Guides](student-guides/)**: Practical resources to help students use AI responsibly, critically, and effectively.
- **[Faculty Guides](faculty-guides/)**: Original and curated guidance on integrating AI into teaching, curriculum development, and assessment.
- **[Prompt Library](prompt-library/)**: Original and curated AI prompts and prompt collections supporting instructional planning, classroom activities, assessment, and professional development.
- **[Classroom Assignments](assignments/)**: Teaching activities and assignment examples that encourage critical thinking, creativity, and meaningful engagement with course material.
- **[Responsible AI](responsible-ai/)**: Resources exploring AI ethics, transparency, privacy, academic integrity, and responsible use.

## Who Is This For?

Educators, students, instructional designers, and anyone interested in thoughtful AI integration in education.

## Contributing

Suggestions and contributions are welcome. Open a GitHub Issue to recommend a topic, share a resource, or suggest an improvement.
