# AI Syllabus Policies Repository

A curated collection of generative AI syllabus policies contributed by educators across academic disciplines.

## About This Collection

This directory catalogs syllabus policies from the *Syllabi Policies for Generative AI Repository*, compiled by Lance Eaton, Ph.D.

The goal is to help educators explore and compare different approaches to generative AI in teaching, learning, assessment, and academic integrity.

## Explore the Policies

Individual syllabus entries are organized in the `policies/` folder. Each entry identifies its contributor, institution, discipline, and reported reuse permissions.
