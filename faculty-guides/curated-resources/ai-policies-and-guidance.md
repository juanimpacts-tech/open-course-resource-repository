### Guiding Principles: Teaching and Learning with AI

**Credit:** Yale University, Poorvu Center for Teaching and Learning  
**Original source:** [Guiding Principles: Teaching and Learning with AI](https://poorvucenter.yale.edu/teaching/teaching-resource-library/ai-guidance-for-teachers)

### Crafting an AI Policy

**Credit:** Yale University, Teaching Development and Initiatives (TDI), August 2024
