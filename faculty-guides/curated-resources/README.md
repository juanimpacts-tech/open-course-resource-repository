# Curated Faculty Resources

A collection of **third-party educational resources** relevant to teaching and learning with and about AI. This catalog includes resources that support AI integration as well as activities that intentionally reduce or avoid AI use.

## Browse by topic

- [AI Policies and Guidance](ai-policies-and-guidance.md) — institutional principles, syllabus policy examples, ethics, and transparency.
- [Course and Assessment Design](course-and-assessment-design.md) — approaches to course design and alternative assessment.
- [Assignment Examples](assignment-examples.md) — practical classroom activities credited to their original authors.

## Attribution and reuse

Each entry identifies the originating author or organization where known and provides a source link. Descriptions are original catalog summaries rather than republications. **Links do not imply endorsement, affiliation, or permission to redistribute** the original work. Third-party PDFs supplied for research were not bundled. If you adapt an activity, credit its creator and check the applicable permissions.

Some linked Google Docs may have access restrictions or be changed by their owners. An unattributed PDF example is explicitly marked as needing author verification.

## For contributors

To suggest a resource, open a GitHub Issue with: title, creator, original URL, a brief description of classroom use, and any reuse/license information.
