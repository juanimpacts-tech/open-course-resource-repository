# Course and Assessment Design

Curated approaches and resource collections on assessment, AI use, and student learning. The summaries below are for discovery and do not reproduce third-party materials.

### Assignment Ideas

**Credit:** Against A.I. (community-contributed resource collection)  
**Original source:** [Assignment Ideas](https://against-a-i.com/assignment-ideas/)

A collection of alternative assessment and classroom activity proposals, including writing, oral assessment, annotation, creative work, reflection, and collaborative learning.

**Catalog note:** The collection credits its individual contributors; the separately cataloged examples below preserve that attribution. The site could not be independently fetched during preparation; descriptions reflect the list supplied by the repository curator.

### Four Frictions: or How To Resist AI in Education

**Credit:** Sonja Drimmer and Christopher J. Nygren, Public Books  
**Original source:** [Four Frictions: or How To Resist AI in Education](https://www.publicbooks.org/four-frictions-or-how-to-resist-ai-in-education/)

An essay examining approaches to resisting AI in educational practice.

**Catalog note:** Listed in Against A.I.’s Assignment Ideas resource collection; consult the original for its argument.

### Assignments That Mitigate AI Abuse

**Credit:** Beth McMurtrie, Teaching newsletter, The Chronicle of Higher Education (July 10, 2025)  
**Original source:** [Assignments That Mitigate AI Abuse](https://against-a-i.com/assignment-ideas/)

A newsletter compilation of instructor-reported approaches, including structured in-class activities, writing workshops, flipped examinations, and teaching a chatbot followed by reflection.

**Catalog note:** The provided PDF reproduces the newsletter. Contributors discussed include Megan B. McNamara and Jason Gulya; credit them when discussing their specific strategies. The link points to the supplied collection, not a verified original newsletter URL.
