# Using AI for Brainstorming

## Learning Objective
Use generative AI to explore ideas while maintaining independent judgment and ownership of your work.

## Before You Begin
Check your instructor's AI-use policy; in the abscence of one check with your instructor. Do not submit AI-generated content as your own work.

## Activity: Explore, Evaluate, Reflect

### 1. Start with Your Own Ideas
Write three ideas about your assignment before using AI.

### 2. Ask AI to Expand Your Thinking
Try this prompt:

"I'm exploring [topic] for a class assignment. Ask me three questions that will help me consider perspectives I may have overlooked. Do not write the assignment for me."

### 3. Evaluate the Suggestions
- Which suggestions were helpful?
- Which suggestions seemed incomplete or inaccurate?
- What important perspectives were missing?

### 4. Make Your Own Decision
Select one direction for your assignment and explain to yourself why you chose it.

## Reflection
How did AI influence your thinking? What decisions did you make independently?

## Responsible Use Reminder
Verify factual claims, protect personal information, follow institutional policies, and disclose AI assistance when required.
