
# How I Use AI: A Teaching Notebook

A living record of my experiences, experiments, and reflections on integrating artificial intelligence into teaching and learning.

## About This Notebook

As both an educator and a professional working with AI, I am interested in how artificial intelligence can support learning without replacing the critical thinking, creativity, and judgment that education seeks to develop.

This notebook documents how I communicate expectations, design learning activities, experiment with AI, and reflect on what works and what doesn't.

Rather than presenting a single prescribed approach, it offers a window into an evolving teaching practice.

- **AI Literacy:** Developing the skills to evaluate and use AI effectively.

- **Reflection:** Documenting experiments, observations, and lessons learned.
